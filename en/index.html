CleanSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Contact: mlxnle@163.com

CleanSnap (山竹) does not collect personal data from you.

  • Photo analysis and cleanup run entirely on your device.
  • Photos are not uploaded to our servers or the cloud for processing.
  • We do not sell your data or use your photos for advertising or third-party AI training.
  • Purchases and restores are handled by Apple; we only receive information needed to unlock in-app features.

Privacy choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no separate online privacy dashboard. You can manage photo library access anytime in Settings.

Terms of Use

Subscriptions and purchases are subject to the Apple Standard EULA.
